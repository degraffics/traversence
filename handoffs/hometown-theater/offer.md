# Hometown Theater × Traversence: a community night in St. Johns

*One page, for the Founder and Sales (14) to hand over in person. Draft for the CMO (7) and Editorial (12).*

**What we'd like to do with you:** one community night at the Hometown Theater during the December run of
*Avengers: Doomsday* (reported to open about Dec 18, 2026; the date is yours). You pick the form:
- **A block of seats** at a regular showing, or
- **A special screening** of your own.

**How tickets sell:** through your own listing on Traversence, as an **Event tickets** item (seats at a date and time,
with a capacity and a ticket checked in at the door). On the Free or Basic plan, buyers pay you directly by the
manual methods you choose (Zelle, bank transfer, check, cash), and you mark each order paid. Traversence takes
**0%**: no fee, no cut of any sale (commercial.md §1). Your regular showtimes stay yours: we show them on the event
page as information only, with no ticket link of ours, and your own box office is untouched.

**What you get, at no cost:**
- Your listing, claimed and yours to edit (it's already on Traversence as listing #8807, waiting for you).
- An event page for the night, linked from your listing and from the St. Johns guide.
- **"Dinner and a movie":** a trail and a magazine-style showcase pairing the night with St. Johns restaurants, so
  guests eat in town before the show.
- **GO Code posters** for your lobby and for each restaurant on the trail, each opening the night's page (5 free codes
  with your account; commercial.md §1.3).
- A story after the night, with your OK on anything we say about you.

**What we ask:** claim your listing, agree the night's date, seats and price, and let us put up the posters.

**The film's title** is used factually only. We use no studio artwork or logos, only your own licensed promotional
material, and only with your permission.

**Next step:** claim your listing at the link on this page's GO Code, or we'll do it together with you today.
