### I'm on the Traversence team. How is a Team role tested before it counts as live?
On a role's page in **System Configuration → Team**, press **Run a test ticket**. It runs on our staging copy only, never on the live site. It makes one test ticket, lets the role take its own step on it once, and shows the result and what it cost ("Passed: … Cost $0.00"). Then the step is undone and the test ticket withdrawn, so nothing is left in the Work queue. The page keeps the last result under **Test ticket**, and **AI access** says what the role's steps call and cost: today, our own code only, at $0. A role counts as live once its test ticket passes. Where a role can't be tested yet, the button is greyed and the page says why and what to do next.
