The two clocks (02, model 1.2)

The run schedule: when work happens

Each workflow's "What starts it" (on its card's page); a task's every N minutes and its share of a run; the bulk loads' night window (1–5 AM Arizona); the site's jobs (Scheduler::JOBS); the 2 AM pre-flight; the watch every 5 minutes.

The diagnostic clock: how a fault is judged

A failing component's check: 3 tries or 60 minutes (control_fix_tries, control_fix_minutes; "Keep the hour"); slow is never a trip; the back-off ladder; late and stuck (a job past its time + 5 min; a run unfinished after 2 hours; W0 silent over 5 min); Pulse's escalation (Critical: the Manager at 2 hours, the Director at 4). A scheduled run is not a try.
