## What it does, each with its source
- **Cost tab** (`app/ui/cost-tab.php`, `js/tv-cost.js`): the price tiers table gets `data-tv-table`, with `tv_table_kit()`
  printed before it (T4's way) and `TvTable.all()` after each redraw as you type, so it takes the standard: the header
  on the section colour, numbers on the right, and a card per run on a phone (§2, §5, §12, §13). The current run stays
  marked, in sage on the card. Its labels' #8A7355 moves onto `--tv-meta` (css-theme-brand §3). Its own table CSS
  (`.cx-tt` widths, padding, borders) gives way to the standard's.
- **Vault's list** (`app/ui/vault.php`, `js/tv-vault.js`, decisions/0156): it keeps its own engine, like T2's Pulse, because
  its list is decrypted in the browser and can't page on the server. Since 2026-11-12 it already had the header
  select-all, sorting, ⚙ Columns, the count, Show N, the page bar, Show more, one search box, the Show filter and the
  bulk bar only when ticked. Changed:
  - headings small, in the meta colour (§4); rows on the card colour, the header on the section colour (§13); 44px
    rows with a sub-line allowed (§5); the hover tint; a ticked row tinted apart from the rest;
  - the list's colours onto the tokens (css-theme-brand §3): 33 values, only in the list's own rules (header, rows,
    sorting, Columns, ticks, pager). The unlock, set-up and recovery screens are left for a tokens pass, and the
    Reused / Not secure flags keep their colours (their meaning isn't a table matter);
  - "Last changed" and "Added" through TvTime with their zone, "Nov 19, 10:04 AM MST" (§5; CLAUDE.md "Times where they
    happened"). They were a bare date with the full time only in the tooltip. `dshort()` has no caller left; it stays,
    since removing a live function needs Jason's OK (the zip check's `--drops`).
