1625: ### Do I charge sales tax on an item?
1626: Each item's kind sets the usual answer, so you rarely touch it: physical goods, made-to-order work, tickets, digital goods, subscriptions and rentals are taxed at your store's rate (set in E-commerce setup); most services, fresh food for the home (harvest) and stays (whose lodging tax is added as its own line) aren't. Change it for any item in **Details** → **Sales tax** (tap the **i** for what each means); when you change an item's kind, its sales tax follows unless you've set it yourself. Your state and town decide what's taxable, so check with your accountant.

1628: ### What kinds of item can my business sell?
1629: Nine, and each one works its own way, everywhere it shows (your catalog, the Marketplace, your storefront, checkout, orders and receipts). In **Catalog**, choose an item's **Kind** (tap the **i** beside the list: a pop-up shows what each kind is, with an example, and you can pick one there), then open its card and tap **Kind details**. The kinds follow the item's name: food like tamales or snickerdoodles can be Physical goods, Made to order, Harvest & perishables or a Subscription, never a service; an animal is Physical goods only; a cabin is a booking or a rental. Kinds that don't fit the name are greyed with why, and words in the name add their own ("Kayak **rental**", "Tamale-making **class**", "Coffee **club**"). Each kind:
1630: - **Physical goods:** weight and size, a stock alert, pickup only if you like.
1631: - **Service / labor:** a flat price bought at checkout (with a travel fee if you charge one), or by the hour, which buyers ask you to quote; the area you serve.
1632: - **Made to order:** the questions buyers answer at checkout ("Size", "Wording"), a deposit paid when ordering (50% unless you change it) with the rest invoiced when it's ready, and a proof they approve first.
1633: - **Event tickets:** the date and time, where, and how many; buyers get a code for each seat to show at the door, and your team checks it in once from the ticket.
1634: - **Digital goods:** the file, kept private (add it on your Marketplace page); buyers get a download link that works a set number of times within a set number of days.
1635: - **Subscriptions & memberships:** charged every month or year until the buyer cancels, with a free trial if you like. Through Stripe for now.
1636: - **Bookings & reservations:** nights at your place; guests choose their dates and number, agree to your house rules (kept as a record), and pay your lodging tax on top.
1637: - **Harvest & perishables:** the pickup windows buyers choose from, and how fresh it is.
1638: - **Rental equipment:** rented by the day, with the dates checked against how many you have, an optional damage waiver, and a deposit, charged and refunded on return, or held on the card for rentals of 6 days or fewer on Stripe. When it comes back, open the order and tap **Mark returned**, keeping any part of the deposit for damage.
1639: The item says what it still needs before it can be sold (a ticket its date, a digital item its file). Square items you bring in keep their kind (Square's appointments, events, digital and memberships). Earlier items carry over: items are Physical goods, classes are Event tickets.

1651: ### How do I build my storefront's pages?
1652: In your business portal, open **Storefront & pages**, then under **Your pages** tap a page's name and **Edit the page**. It opens in the builder, the same one our own pages use: press **Edit** on the page, and a panel opens (from the bottom on a phone, at the side on a computer). **Add** holds the blocks: a heading, words (paragraphs, lists, **bold**, *italic*, links), a picture, a button, a divider and a spacer; tap one to add it, or drag it into place. **Layout** shows the sections (one, two or three columns) and lets you drag blocks and sections around; **Design** sets alignment, width and spacing for a computer, a tablet and a phone, each on its own. Pictures you upload are kept in your Gallery and count toward your plan's storage. A page you haven't built yet starts from the text you wrote for it, as one block, and that text is kept as it was. **Save draft** keeps your changes for your team only: you see them in the builder and through **Preview**, while visitors still see what's published. **Publish** puts it on your storefront as the next version; ▾ → **Versions** lists every one, with who and when, and **Roll back** puts any earlier one back. Who can do what is set by your roles in **Team & roles**: **Storefront & theme** to build and save drafts, **Publish** to publish, with no one asked to approve; for a role without Publish, the button is greyed and says why. Your storefront stays plain and fast: it runs no scripts, so the builder's parts of the site (a search box, a map, likes and comments, a form) aren't offered on it. Your pages take your storefront's theme (see the next question). Coming next: blocks for selling (your items, collections, a featured item, hours, map and directions, a booking or quote form, reviews), and the builder's bar on your preview. Publishing at a set time isn't offered for storefronts yet.

1682: ### What can my business sell on the Marketplace?

1697: ### Why is Traversence asking for my business's tax ID and bank details?
1698: A federal law, the INFORM Consumers Act, asks every online marketplace to collect and verify the details of a "high-volume" seller: one with 200 or more sales and $5,000 or more in a year. Once your Marketplace sales reach that (your Trust score page shows how close you are), we ask, and you have 10 days to give your legal name, address, phone, email and tax ID, in **Market 360° → Trust score → INFORM Act details**. Your bank account counts as verified through Stripe or Square once payouts are on. We ask again each year. Your tax ID is kept encrypted and never shown to buyers. Past $20,000 a year, the law shows buyers your business's name, address and contact on your items (only your state if it's a home address). If the details aren't in by the deadline, card checkout pauses until they are; your listing and pages stay up.
