Its menu has everything in one place: Overview, its Pulse and messages, the listing and Edit listing, then **Market 360°**, one button for everything about selling (its pages are in the **Market 360°** menu at the top of each: **Get selling** first, then six modules: **Business Identification** (Trust score, Legal identity), **Money & Processing** (E-commerce setup, Sales tax, Store policies), **Products & Inventory** (Catalog, Marketplace, Bundles), **E-commerce** (Orders, Customers, Quotes & invoices, Shipping & pickup, Discount codes, Reports), **Storefront** (Storefront & pages) and **Marketing** (Linked people, Marketing); pages still coming show greyed, saying what they'll do), then its other tools, then Team & roles and Plan & settings. **Get selling** walks you through setting up to sell in seven parts that tick themselves as you finish them: business profile, store, catalog and inventory, getting paid, shipping and tax, verification and payouts (done in Stripe's or Square's own setup; it ticks once Stripe or Square says payments and payouts are on, and lists what they still need), then test and launch. Open it by choosing the business under **Your listings** in your account menu, or at traversence.com/business/. Each person sees what their role allows (Owner, Manager, Accountant, Sales, Support, Creator, Developer or Viewer, who can look but not change anything), and each tool is opened by your plan: one your plan doesn't include shows greyed with the plan it comes with. Customers, Catalog, Marketplace, E-commerce setup, Store policies, Storefront & pages, Trust score, Linked people, Quotes & invoices, Orders, Discount codes, Sales tax, Shipping & pickup and Plan & settings (with your team) are open now; the inbox, legal identity, bundles, marketing (QR codes, short links, campaigns) and reports are coming. Your business's information is kept in its own place, walled off from every other business: no other business can reach it, whatever happens.
