Question 3 of 4

Usage analytics

Let us count how Traversence is used (pages visited, searches that found nothing, what's hard to use) with an analytics service, so we can fix and improve it.

ⓘ What this means

With Usage analytics on, an analytics service we use measures how the site is used: which pages are visited, how people move between them, the kind of device and browser, and roughly where (city level, from the internet address). It sets its own cookies to tell a returning visit from a new one.

We use what it shows us only to improve Traversence. We don't sell it, we don't use it for advertising, and we don't let the analytics service use it for its own advertising.

Off by default. Everything on Traversence works the same either way. Change it any time on the Privacy page or in Privacy Settings → Your tools.

Off unless you tick it. Change it any time in Privacy Settings.

Question 4 of 4

Videos from YouTube

Play videos that come from YouTube or Vimeo right on the page. Playing one lets YouTube (Google) or Vimeo see your visit and set their cookies.

ⓘ What this means

Some places, listings and journeys include a video hosted on YouTube or Vimeo. We load them in YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode, but once a video plays, YouTube (Google) or Vimeo receives your internet address and the page you're on, and may set cookies, under their own privacy policies.

With Videos on, videos load as part of the page. With it off, each video shows a Play button and loads only when you tap it, or you can open it on YouTube instead.

Change it any time on the Privacy page or in Privacy Settings → Your tools.

Off unless you tick it. Change it any time in Privacy Settings.
