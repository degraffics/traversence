## How will I hear about an order you made for me?

By email, every time: when it's ready for your review, when we update it, when it's in production, sent to print,
shipped or ready for pickup, when it's complete, and if it's cancelled. These emails can't be turned off, because they're
about your order.

If you have a Traversence account with the same email address as the order, each of these also lands in your Pulse
(your inbox on the site), so you can find them all in one place. The email links back to your order.

## Why does my order email say I can't stop it?

Emails about an order you placed with us are part of the order, so there's no "Stop these" link on them. You can still
choose, in Dashboard → App Settings → Communications, how everything else reaches you. Text messages about orders are
coming; they aren't sent yet.

## Do I get a push notification about my order?

When your invoice is due and you've allowed notifications on a device, yes. For the order's other updates (its stages),
push is off unless you turn it on in Communications. Your email and your Pulse always have them.

## When will I hear that my invoice is due?

As soon as it's due, by email with the link to pay, and in your Pulse if you have an account with the order's email
address.
