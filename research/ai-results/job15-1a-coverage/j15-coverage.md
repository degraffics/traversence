**Method and caveats**
- I used the category names only, so I can't see what is actually in each listing. A name can hide a service (a "Convenience Stores" listing may sell fuel, "Medical Centers" may offer urgent care), so "thin" often means "can't confirm".
- "Missing" means no category plausibly covers the kind. "Thin" means there are zero or one dedicated listings, or only a loosely related category.
- Towns with 5 or more listings are included. Excluded: St John'S (1), St Johns (3) and St. Johns (2). These look like misspellings of Saint Johns, and they add a clinic, a nurse practitioner and a health-services listing to it.
- Pinetop Lakeside (5) is probably a merged spelling of Pinetop and Lakeside. Mcnary (9) and Sun Valley (5) are nearly empty. Their gaps may be a data issue rather than real ones.

**Biggest gaps for visitors, ranked**
1. **Fuel in Springerville, Eagar and Snowflake.** None has a Gas Stations category. Springerville has no convenience category either, so fuel looks missing there. These are the highest-impact gaps for travelers.
2. **Lodging in Saint Johns, Snowflake, Eagar and Taylor.** There are no hotels or motels in the first three, and only one in Taylor. Lodging is solid in Show Low, Holbrook, Pinetop and Lakeside.
3. **Urgent care and hospital access.** Urgent Care appears only in Show Low and Lakeside, and ER only in Show Low and Springerville. Holbrook, Pinetop, Snowflake, Eagar, Saint Johns and Taylor list doctors and dentists but no urgent care or ER. Eagar also has no pharmacy category.
4. **Groceries.** Every town except Springerville, Snowflake and Pinetop has one or zero Grocery Stores, even Show Low and Holbrook. Eagar and Taylor have none and rely on convenience or variety stores.
5. **Near-empty entries (Mcnary, Pinetop Lakeside, Sun Valley).** These are probably listings filed under the wrong town name. I would clean up the town names and merge the Saint Johns spelling variants before judging coverage there.
