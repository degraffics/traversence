### 0035 Internal Billing System — Subscriptions, Services, and Non-User Counterparties

- **Date:** 2026-09-25. **Index status:** `accepted` (no "Replaced by", 0 files named).
- **Built?** Decided only: "the schema/workflow build-out itself is a separate, later technical-design pass";
  "Concretely still undesigned, flagged here rather than assumed: a `subscriptions` table and a `services` table … a
  `counterparties` table for non-user payees, and the admin-facing view".

**Decides about orders**
- §Decision, Transaction System: "A distinct internal Transaction System, separate from Market and from Quote." For
  Traversence's own accounts receivable (partner payments) and accounts payable (vendor/contractor payments); "never
  through the front-facing, no-processing Market mechanism (`decisions/0031`) and never modeled as a Quote between two
  platform users (`decisions/0032`)". Market and Quote keep "no in-platform payment processing for user-to-user Market
  activity".
- Subscriptions: monthly or annual, "tied to a single listing and a tier (Core/Strategic/Cornerstone) — confirmed per
  direction 2026-09-25: per listing, not per account". Multi-location business = one Subscription per location,
  "each independently priced and independently tiered" (commercial.md §10).
- Subscription fields: **Source** (paid, promotional/comped, trial; generalizes 0034's grant source); **Description**
  (tier, effective from); **Terms** (cadence monthly/annual, rate, multi-location volume-discount band); **Status**
  ("active, past-due, paused, canceled, or (for a promotional grant) pending review", `entities.status`-style enum);
  **Process/management** ("create, renew, convert (promotional → paid or vice versa), or cancel"; 0034's review-gated
  continuation governs a promotional Subscription's transition only).
- Services: separate object from Subscriptions for one-time and ongoing add-on work (White-Glove Setup; Google
  Maps/Local Place Management add-on "$125 one-time + $50/mo per location"; custom multi-location onboarding); billed
  as one-time, per-location or mixed; same four fields (source, description, terms, status).
- Trust/review tie-in "for internal/admin purposes only": billing standing visible to admins beside trust/review
  history. Hard boundary (0007): "a Subscription's status (paid, past-due, comped) must never become a ranking input",
  and billing status never influences public review/rating standing.
- Counterparty: "its own lightweight record type — a **Counterparty** — distinct from both" `users` and `entities`,
  for payees with no platform account (vendors, contractors, Regional Directors, Field Executives, Creative &
  Fulfillment Services providers, commercial.md §5). Accountability unchanged: the staff member who recorded/approved
  a payment is stamped with `actor_user_id` (0002).

**Built (per the text):** none.
**Not built / later:** `subscriptions`, `services`, `counterparties` tables; admin view joining billing status with
trust/review; commercial.md section for Subscriptions and Services.

### 0098 One order system: orders, payments, refunds, deposits, chargebacks, subscriptions

- **Date:** 2026-10-16 (addenda to 2026-11-01). **Index status:** `accepted` (2 files named).
- **Built?** Partly built. Header: "Accepted (Jason, 2026-10-16); the build order is at the end". Section "Decision
  (proposed)" then "Decided (Jason, 2026-10-16)". "Built (2026-10-16): step 1"; "Built (2026-10-16): refunds both ways
  with QuickBooks"; "Built (2026-10-16): the card on our checkout page, and the Sandbox / Production switch". Step 1's
  "**Not yet:** credits taken off at checkout on the site …, staff draft orders and deposits, products with options
  and vendors (step 2)". Build steps 2–5 have no "Built" section in this record.

**Decides about orders**
- Jason's brief: "refunds, deposits, chargebacks are a part of that system … o2o products and subscriptions that will
  be piped through this system, so we need to ensure it is not just GO Codes but order processing."
- §1 One order model: `products` (key, name, kind `one_time`/`subscription`/`service`/`o2o`, price, cadence, taxable,
  QuickBooks item, and a **fulfiller**: "GO Codes issues codes; a plan turns on a tier for a listing; an o2o product
  issues a redeemable voucher; a service opens a work item. New products plug in a fulfiller; the order flow doesn't
  change"). `orders` (who: member, guest by private key, or listing; status, totals, tax, billing details, QuickBooks
  invoice, access link). `order_lines` (product, quantity, price, what was delivered). "`qr_orders` becomes a view or
  is migrated into `orders`; GO Codes' pages keep working."
- §2 Ledger: "A payment ledger, never edited, only added to." Each row: amount (+/−), method, QuickBooks reference,
  date, who recorded it, kind: `charge` (invoice paid, polling or webhook); `refund` (full or partial; our button or a
  QuickBooks RefundReceipt/credit memo); `chargeback`, `chargeback_reversed` ("recorded by staff from QuickBooks
  Payments' notice (button), with the dispute reference"); `deposit` ("when QuickBooks moves the money into the bank
  … net of processing fees"). "An order's state comes from its ledger: paid, partly refunded, refunded, disputed,
  chargeback lost / won."
- §3 Refund/chargeback: staff choose full or partial and a reason; refund made in QuickBooks (RefundReceipt via API,
  tax reversed); ledger records; buyer emailed; "the product's fulfiller undoes what it can": GO Codes leave the
  account, a plan's tier ends, an o2o voucher is voided. "A chargeback does the same when it's lost."
- §4 Subscriptions (0035, per listing): each period an invoice in QuickBooks, emailed; "**QuickBooks AutoPay**"; card
  data stays with Intuit; "Past due after N days → reminders → the tier pauses"; Stripe is the upgrade path if
  automatic renewals/dunning become a must.
- §5 Admin → Sales → Orders replaces "GO Code orders": every order of every kind; filters (kind, status, date); ledger;
  buttons Check payment, Refund (full/partial), Record chargeback, Record chargeback outcome, Resend receipt, Cancel
  unpaid; numbers sales, refunds, chargebacks, net, tax collected, deposits.
- §6 "QuickBooksPay stays the only code that talks to QuickBooks"; gains refund receipts, deposits, recurring
  invoices; every call and webhook logged with its intuit_tid.
- Update (O2O): O2O = "Traversence's own print-on-demand and agency line" (starter kits, banners, curb signs, cards,
  mailers, apparel, swag, design and video packs). Needs products with options (size, material, finish, quantity
  runs, text, artwork upload, GO Codes to print) with price changes per option, "added in the admin without new
  code"; production routing (proof/pre-press, in production, at a vendor, shipped or ready for pickup) with vendors
  (0035 Counterparty) "while the order keeps its status from start to finish"; shipping (EasyPost/ShipStation later;
  flat rates or local pickup first); tax stays QuickBooks AST.
- Update (admin area): "Not in Sales: its own admin area, 'E-Commerce'": Orders · Customers · Products · Production &
  fulfillment · Subscriptions · Promotions · Payments · Reports.
- Update (three kinds, one record): "Every order has the same record (who, what, lines, status, history) … the
  **money** is what differs". Kind 1 Traversence's sales (QuickBooks, our ledger, Admin → E-Commerce); kind 2 business
  storefronts ("**The business's own processor**, paid to them directly, 0% commission … **no money ledger of ours,
  never custody**"; business dashboard, admin read-only); kind 3 member marketplace ("**None through us**: settled
  offline"; both mark complete; disputes in Admin → Safety). "Refunds, chargebacks and deposits exist only for kind 1".
  Business processor choice "is decided with the storefront build".
- Decided 1: "**Payments: QuickBooks.** All of Traversence's own sales (kind 1), subscriptions included … AutoPay for
  recurring."
- Decided 2: admin area "E-Commerce", out of Sales.
- Decided 3 "**Fix it, credit, then refund.**": fix (redo, reprint, replace) → full/partial store credit → refund
  "only when neither works". "**Made-to-order and custom products are final once the order (proof) is approved**";
  we fix our errors, production defects, shipping damage, "never a change of mind or a mistake in what was approved".
  Ledger adds `credit_issued`, `credit_used` ("an account balance per customer, applied at checkout"); every line gets
  **approved at** (proof approval; for a GO Code, its issue), after which final. Order page offers in order: **Fix**

- Decided 4: refunded/charged-back GO Code "becomes inactive, and its link shows a 404"; paid link codes through

- Decided 4a (supersedes "link codes only"): "**Every GO Code routes through TRVGO**"; every scan counted; **Static** =

  unit price and cost, approved_at), `order_ledger` (charge, deposit, refund, credit_issued, credit_used, chargeback,
  chargeback_reversed; unique `request_key`; append-only), `order_events` (history with who). `qr_orders` rows moved
