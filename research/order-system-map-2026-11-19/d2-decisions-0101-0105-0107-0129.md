## 1. Decision 0101: Orders we build for a client, and the product catalog

### 1.0 The base decision (2026-10-16)

- **Status line:** "Status: accepted (Jason, 2026-10-16). Builds on 0098 (the order system) and 0099 (E-Commerce
  operations)." Body written as working behavior ("How it works"); a **Not yet** list names what isn't built.
- **Context:** Jason builds orders for clients ("business cards with a QR, design work, GO Codes"); the client reviews
  and approves them, "proofs included, before anything is made or charged."
- **Decides about orders:**
  - **Payment, chosen per order:** "in full on approval, a deposit on approval and the rest before delivery, or approve
    now and pay the invoice later."
  - **Printed products are made by a vendor;** "Jason places the order with them himself and marks the order's stage.
    (A purchase order sent by the system can come later.)"
  - **Prices** "come from the catalog and can be changed on any order (the difference shows as a discount)."
  - **QuickBooks** "sees the order only when the client approves (an invoice then, as checkout does), not an Estimate
    before."
  - **Catalog** (E-Commerce → Products, `products`): name, code, kind, description, options ("Paper: Matte, Glossy"),
    "a price and cost for each quantity (a quantity between tiers is priced from the tier below)", taxable, vendor,
    turnaround, shown or hidden. "Its QuickBooks product is found or made by name on the first invoice."
  - **Building** (E-Commerce → Client orders → New order for a client, `admin/client-order.php`): the client ("a member
    is linked by email; no account needed"); lines: "a catalog product with quantity and options, a GO Code made with
    what it holds, or a custom line"; "a proof per line (PDF or image, kept outside the web root, a new version per
    round, history kept)"; terms (payment, deposit %); a note; how many days the link works. **Send** emails the private
    link `/order.php?t=…`; **Send again** after changes.
  - **Client's page:** every line with price ("a discount shows the catalog price struck through") and its proof;
    **Approve this proof** or **Ask for changes** ("our team is emailed; the order shows 'Changes requested'").
    "With every proof approved: billing address + name + 'I approve' → the QuickBooks invoice is made (tax from the
    address); the approval is recorded with name, email and IP in the order's history."
  - **Paying:** "the card form from checkout, for the amount due now (the deposit, then the rest; or the whole). The
    ledger records a deposit and later the charge; the order stays 'waiting for payment' until it's paid in full
    (`Orders::settle` keeps a part-paid order pending)." **Paid on QuickBooks' page instead:** "the webhook or 'Check
    payment' records the rest (`QrIssue::markPaid` hands client orders to `ClientOrders::paidInQuickBooks`)."
  - **After payment:** GO Codes issued through trvgo.us, downloaded from the client's page; printed products "move
    through In production → Ordered from the printer → Shipped / Ready for pickup → Complete, each emailing the client
    (with a tracking number). With invoice-later terms, production starts on approval."
  - Refunds, credits, chargebacks, history stay on `admin/order.php`. Staff roles: E-Commerce's (Owner, Accountant;
    0100).
  - **Not yet:** "Purchase orders emailed to the vendor with the approved proof files"; "A short trvgo.us address for
    the client's link"; "Shipping charges as their own line type (a custom line for now)."
- **Changed by:** stages renamed/extended (item statuses, 2026-10-16 pass 1; "Work order issued", 2026-10-28); stage
  emails made opt-in (`notify_progress`, 2026-10-28); "With invoice-later terms, production starts on approval" vs. the
  payment hold (2026-10-28: "an approved order waiting on payment stays out of production … until our team records the
  money"; "Release to production before payment" for trusted clients); purchase orders → work orders (2026-10-28);
  shipping line type (same-day addendum 1); two lists → one (2026-10-16 "One list"); `admin/client-order.php` →
  `/admin/backoffice/orders/<id>` (2026-10-27 later); 0130 ("Order lines keep a snapshot"); 0128 (pull from QuickBooks).

### 1.1 Addendum (2026-10-16): priced like the print vendor quotes

- **Status words:** none; present tense ("The catalog now matches").
- **Decides:**
  - Vendor quotes: "a cost per run size (25 … 2,500), turnaround as a share of the run price (+10% two days, +30% next
    day…), flat extras (PDF proof +$5.00, samples +$9.99) and shipping per shipment."
  - **Run sizes** = rows of vendor cost and our price; "our price left empty is the cost plus the product's **markup %**
    (stored with the rows: `tiers = {"markup", "rows"}`; older plain lists still read). The form shows the margin."
  - **Choices can carry a price:** "`Turnaround: 4 Business Days, 2 Business Days +10%` (a % of the run's price and
    cost) or `PDF proof: No, Yes +5.00` (a vendor cost, marked up). **The first choice is the standard one.** Fixed specs
    (size, stock, colors, coating) go in the description." (This is the only option-default rule in these files: the
    first listed choice is the standard; no other rule on option defaults when adding a product appears in 0101/0105/
    0106/0107/0129.)
  - **Turnaround** is an option choice priced as % of the run's price and cost (catalog field "turnaround" in base).
  - **Shipping** "is its own line on a client order (method, the vendor's charge, what the client pays, ship-to, not
    taxed unless ticked); it's not a production item." Custom lines "take an optional cost for margins."
  - Forms show the page's own missing-field messages, not browser bubbles.
- **Changed by:** shipping line → per-item shipping (pass 2) → in-place ship icon (edited in place) → manifests
  (2026-10-21). Pricing model extended by 0130 (recipes, COGS, "The order pulls from the catalog… The order can override
  any price or cost for itself").
